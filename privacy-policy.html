Privacy Policy

LingoSphere AI Language Tutor • Effective Date: November 27, 2026
Summary of Our Commitment:

LingoSphere is committed to protecting your privacy. We process speech and voice audio solely for live language tutoring and AI conversation practice. We never sell your personal data or speech recordings to third parties.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: When you sign in with Google or Gmail, we store your User ID, Display Name, and Email address to manage your learning progress.
  • Audio & Speech Data: Real-time microphone audio is converted to text to enable two-way conversation with the AI Tutor. Audio streams are processed ephemerally during active sessions and are not stored permanently.
  • Learning Progress & Activity: Lessons completed, vocabulary flashcards (Leitner box progression), daily streaks, speaking turns, and quiz scores.
  • Subscription Data: Purchase tokens and subscription tier (Free or Premium) verified via Google Play Billing.
  • Advertising & Device Identifiers (Free Tier): For non-subscribed users, Google AdMob may collect the Android Advertising ID, IP address, app interaction events, and coarse diagnostic data to serve and measure ads.

2. How We Use Information

  • To deliver real-time AI voice calling and customized language instruction in Spanish, French, German, Japanese, and other target languages.
  • To personalize learning curriculum based on your selected proficiency level (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced).
  • To generate post-call learning reports including vocabulary learned and grammar corrections.
  • To enforce daily speaking call limits and display ads for free-tier users (ads are disabled for active Premium subscribers).

3. Advertising & Google AdMob

LingoSphere uses Google AdMob (authorized via our app-ads.txt seller record) to support the free tier of the application. Google and its partners may use device identifiers and cookies to deliver personalized or non-personalized advertisements and prevent ad fraud. You can reset or opt out of personalized advertising anytime in Android Settings → Google → Ads, or review Google's Advertising Privacy Policy.

4. Audio & Voice Data Processing

Voice audio is captured using standard Android Audio APIs and processed with Google Cloud / Gemini AI APIs solely for the purpose of conversational transcription, language evaluation, and real-time response generation. Audio data is encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS.

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain your user profile, learning statistics, and flashcards for as long as your account is active. You have the right to request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time:

  • In-App Deletion: Open LingoSphere → Login Screen or Profile → "Delete Account & All Data".
  • Web Deletion Request: Visit our public deletion request page: Data Deletion Request Portal.

6. Security of Your Information

We protect user data using Google Firebase Firestore zero-trust security rules, end-to-end HTTPS encryption, and tokenized Google Identity Authentication.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or your personal data, please contact the developer at gadgetexplorerpro@gmail.com.
